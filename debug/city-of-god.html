City of God

2002

Cidade de Deus

Directed by Fernando Meirelles

Synopsis

If you run, the beast catches you; if you stay, the beast eats you.

In the poverty-stricken favelas of Rio de Janeiro in the 1970s, two young men choose different paths. Rocket is a budding photographer who documents the increasing drug-related violence of his neighborhood, while José “Zé” Pequeno is an ambitious drug dealer diving into a dangerous life of crime.

There are several relevant characters introduced throughout the story and yet, it ingeniously follows the journey of every single one of them. The direction is as honest as it is ruthless, the script is uncompromising with its content, cinematography is raw, evocative & stylish, editing is…

This might just be one of the most impactful pieces of cinema I have ever seen. The amount of different emotions I have felt in a 2-hour period is unbelievable; the majority of films with this approach tend to leave me overwhelmed. This was definitely not one of those films. The stylistic camera work is complete genius. Every character is unforgettable. The pacing is phenomenal, and all three acts are just as good as each other.
 
Some of the lines and dialogue in this film are so well written, to the point where I was actually shocked…
