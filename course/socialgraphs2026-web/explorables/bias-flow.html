How one version of the world becomes the next

Read from top to bottom. Each step can change what the next step is able to represent.

1 World as it should and could be
What we would like to study

Imagine the full conversation or population we actually care about, with all relevant voices represented. This is the reference point we would like our analysis to speak about.

↓ retrospective injustice
and societal bias
2 World as it is
What actually happens

People do not have equal access, visibility or opportunity to speak. Existing social inequalities already shape who gets heard, published or recorded.

↓ sampling and
measurement
3 World according to data
What our dataset contains

We only observe the texts we managed to collect. Platform access, sampling, language filters, missing data, annotation and measurement choices decide what is present and what is absent.

↓ questions asked and
outcomes chosen
4 World according to model
What the model sees

The model sees the data we collected and the target we gave it. A topic, sentiment score, entity label or ranking therefore also carries the choices that created its input.
